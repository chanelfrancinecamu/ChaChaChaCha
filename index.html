Discover the Philippines

The Philippines is an archipelagic country in Southeast Asia situated in the western Pacific Ocean. It consists of more than 7,600 islands, broadly categorized under three main geographical divisions from north to south: Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.

Key Highlights

  • Capital City: Manila (with Quezon City as the most populous city).
  • Languages: Filipino and English are the official languages, alongside over 170 regional languages.
  • Natural Wonders: Famous for its biodiversity, white-sand beaches like Boracay and Palawan, active volcanoes like Mayon, and the ancient Banaue Rice Terraces.
  • Culture: A vibrant blend of indigenous Austronesian traditions with strong Spanish, American, and Asian influences, celebrated through colorful festivals (fiestas) and warm hospitality.

Known globally as the "Pearl of the Orient Seas," the Philippines offers a rich tapestry of history, tropical landscapes, and bustling modern metropolises.
